# Sign of AI Writing — Raw Input Dataset

## Phần I. Các dấu hiệu cấu trúc, nhịp điệu, lập luận và đặc thù tiếng Việt
1. Độ biến thiên chiều dài câu cực thấp (Low Burstiness – Nhịp văn "máy đếm nhịp")
2. Hiện tượng "Đẳng cấu cú pháp" (Syntactic Isomorphism – Khác từ nhưng trùng khuôn câu)
3. Cấu trúc "Đoạn văn đóng hộp" (Boxy, Self-Contained Paragraphs)
4. Trọng số luận điểm phẳng và Mật độ thông tin đồng đều (Uniform Information Density / Flat Epistemic Weight)
5. Đường cong lập luận kiểu "Con lắc an toàn" (Pendulum Hedging)
6. Sự phá vỡ nhịp điệu bản ngữ Tiếng Việt (Cấu trúc Đề - Thuyết, Tỉnh lược Zero-anaphora, và Tắc nghẽn âm tiết Hán - Việt Syllabic Congestion)

## Phần II. Các dấu hiệu từ Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing (Field Guide)
- Content:
  - Statistical regression to the mean / generic smoothing
  - Undue emphasis on significance, legacy, and broader trends (WP:AILEGACY, WP:AITREND)
  - Canned emphasis on notability, attribution, and media coverage (WP:OVERATTRIBUTION, WP:AIATTR)
  - Superficial analyses (WP:SUPERFICIAL - present participle "-ing" phrases at sentence end)
  - Promotional and advertisement-like language (WP:AIPUFFERY, WP:AIPEACOCK)
  - Vague expression of connection or association (WP:AICONNECT, WP:AIASSOCIATION)
  - Vague attributions and overgeneralization of opinions (WP:AIWEASEL)
  - Outline-like conclusions about challenges and future prospects (WP:FACESCHALLENGES)
  - "Awards and recognition" section (X and Y headers)
- Language and grammar:
  - High density of "AI vocabulary" words (WP:AIVOCAB, WP:AIWORDS)
  - Avoidance of basic copulatives ("is"/"are" phrases -> serves as, stands as, features, offers, refers to) (WP:AINOCOPULA, WP:AIREPRESENTS)
  - Negative parallelisms (WP:AIPARALLEL: Not just X, but also Y; Not X, but Y; Y rather than X)
  - Leads treating Wikipedia lists or broad article titles as proper nouns
  - Rule of three (WP:RO3)
- Style:
  - Title case in section headings (WP:AITITLECASE)
  - Overuse of boldface (WP:AIBOLD)
  - Inline-header vertical lists (WP:AILIST)
  - Heading errors (Title heading, Skipping heading levels, Overuse of level 1 headings, Headings only containing other headings)
  - Emoji as formatting (WP:AIEMOJI)
  - Unusual use of tables (WP:AITABLE)
  - Curly quotation marks and apostrophes (WP:AICURLY)
- Communication intended for the user:
  - Collaborative communication (WP:CERTAINLY, WP:COLLABCOMM)
  - Hedging disclaimers about source availability and recommended usage (WP:AIDISCLAIMER)
  - Phrasal templates and placeholder text (WP:AIPLACEHOLDER)
- Markup & Citations:
  - Use of Markdown (WP:MARKDOWN, WP:AIMARKDOWN, WP:AIASTERISK, WP:AIHASHTAG, WP:AILINK, thematic breaks, broken wikitext)
  - Internal formatting and reference markup bugs (WP:OAICITE, WP:STARTSPAN, WP:GROKCARD, DeepSeek lenticular brackets, Perplexity attached_file, :::writing)
  - Non-existent or out-of-place categories (WP:AIREDCAT) & Non-existent templates
  - Citations (WP:AIFICTREF: broken external links, invalid DOI/ISBNs, DOIs leading to unrelated articles, book citations without page numbers, WP:AICITESTYLE, utm_source=, unused named references)
- Comment & Edit Summary & Miscellaneous indicators:
  - WP:AICOMMENT, WP:AISUMMARY (canned assurance of adherence, mention of preserved/retained info, overemphasis on citations/parameters)
  - Pronounced shift in writing style, Submission statements, Pre-placed maintenance templates (WP:AIDECLINE), Canned user pages (WP:AIUSERPAGE), Permissions gaming (WP:PGAME), Pro-authoritarian bias
- Historical indicators:
  - Statements about knowledge cutoffs (WP:AICUTOFF)
  - Didactic disclaimers (WP:DIDACTIC)
  - Section summaries (WP:CONCLUSION, WP:INCONCLUSION)
  - Prompt refusal
  - Overuse of em dashes (WP:AIDASH) & subsequent punctuation flattening (post-Sept 2026)
  - Abrupt cut offs, Outdated access-date parameters
  - Lexical diversity / elegant variation (WP:AIELEVAR)
